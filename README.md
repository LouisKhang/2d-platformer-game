---

## CONTROLS

### Movement

* A – Move left
* D – Move right
* W – Jump / Move up
* S – Crouch / Move down

---

### Combat

* J – Shoot
* K – Melee attack
* L – Dash

---

### Skills

* U – Throw bomb
* I – Activate shield

---

### Interaction

* G – Pull lever
* Y – Interact with objects:

  * Activate tomb to summon an elevator
  * Enter a portal
  * Board a boat
  * Leave a boat

Note: Interaction actions only work when the player is close to the object.

## GAME OBJECTIVE

* Progress through levels

* Use skills strategically to survive
